# Rochak Badyal

**PhD student in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Minnesota Twin Cities**

I work on the part between a sensor reading and a useful decision.

My research is focused on state estimation, sensor fusion, and control for agricultural robots. A question I keep coming back to is simple to state but difficult to solve: when measurements are noisy, incomplete, or disagree with one another, how can a machine still understand what it is doing?

## What I am working on

Right now, I am a Research Assistant in the Laboratory for Innovations in Sensing, Estimation and Control, advised by [Professor Rajesh Rajamani](https://cse.umn.edu/dsi/rajesh-rajamani). I develop state-estimation algorithms for robots operating in agricultural environments.

My interests include:

- state estimation and sensor fusion
- control systems and system dynamics
- robotics in outdoor and field environments
- vehicle dynamics
- modeling and simulation in MATLAB and Python

## Before the PhD

Before graduate school, I spent four years at Maruti Suzuki R&D working on automotive fuel-tank systems, from early design and development through mass production.

That experience still shapes the way I approach research. I appreciate a clean model, but I also care about what happens when the model meets real hardware, imperfect measurements, manufacturing constraints, and operating conditions that were not in the original plan.
